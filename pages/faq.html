Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding medication dispensing regulations, prescription uploads, delivery areas, and returns.

Simply search for your required medication, add it to your shopping cart, and proceed to checkout. During checkout, you will be prompted to upload a clear photo or scan of your valid doctor's prescription. Our licensed pharmacist team will review the dosage and authenticity before packaging and dispatching.

Within the Greater Colombo area, standard orders are delivered within 4 to 24 hours. For outstation regions across Sri Lanka, delivery takes 1 to 2 business days. All temperature-sensitive medications are transported in specialized insulated thermal boxes.

All orders with a subtotal of Rs. 5,000 or more qualify automatically for FREE standard delivery across Sri Lanka.

Yes, all diagnostic devices (such as Omron blood pressure monitors, Roche Accu-Chek glucose meters, and Beurer pulse oximeters) are 100% genuine and imported directly with official manufacturer warranties (1 to 3 years depending on the brand).

Under Sri Lankan NMRA regulations, prescription medicines cannot be returned once dispensed, to prevent tampering and ensure safety. However, defective medical equipment or incorrectly dispatched items can be returned within 7 days in unopened original packaging.
